Sustained observations in remote environments require reliable instruments, power, communications and data systems. Developing those capabilities is a research challenge in its own right.

## GAMB2LE and the AURORA observatory

**GAMB2LE** is a principal focus of this work. Its **AURORA** platform is designed to bring remote sensing, in situ instruments and uncrewed aircraft together with renewable power to study the Greenland ice–atmosphere interface. [Explore GAMB2LE and AURORA]({{ '/projects/gamb2le/' | relative_url }}).

## An end-to-end approach

We connect the scientific question to the complete observing system: what to measure, how to deploy it, how to monitor performance and how to produce data that researchers can use.

AURORA's development makes these connections tangible. Its power system controls battery charging and energy distribution; its science system controls instruments, collects observations and transfers data. The physical structure must also support field installation, transport, snow accumulation and strong winds. The team uses modular aluminium frames to balance strength, weight and ease of assembly. [Read the February 2026 engineering update](https://www.gamb2le.co.uk/latest-news/aurora-development).

The first test deployment at the University of Leeds farm brought these systems together in April 2026. Testing an integrated observatory gives the team experience operating the measurement chain as a whole. [See the first field report](https://www.gamb2le.co.uk/latest-news/first-aurora-test-deployment).

## Building on Greenland field experience

This approach builds on experience with **ICECAPS-MELT**, an autonomous, solar- and wind-powered platform deployed at Raven Camp, Greenland, in 2024. Its measurements connect conditions within the snow and firn to the atmosphere above, helping researchers understand the processes accompanying surface melt. [Explore ICECAPS-MELT](https://www.icecapsmelt.org/).

Field observations become useful beyond a deployment when they are documented and archived. The [ICECAPS-MELT snow-property collection](https://catalogue.ceda.ac.uk/uuid/aef72bc18a244465a721646babf3018a/) preserves manual and instrumented snow profiles from the May and August 2024 visits. Our [data page]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}) connects these observations with other research archives.
