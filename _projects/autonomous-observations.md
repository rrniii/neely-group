Sustained observations in remote environments require reliable instruments, power, communications and data systems. Developing those capabilities is a research challenge in its own right.

## GAMB2LE and the AURORA observatory

**GAMB2LE** is a principal focus of this work. Its **AURORA** platform brings remote sensing, in situ instruments and uncrewed aircraft together with renewable power to study the Greenland ice–atmosphere interface. [Explore GAMB2LE and AURORA]({{ '/projects/gamb2le/' | relative_url }}).

## An end-to-end approach

We connect the scientific question to the complete observing system: what to measure, how to deploy it, how to monitor performance and how to produce data that researchers can use.

This approach builds on experience with **ICECAPS-MELT**, an autonomous, solar- and wind-powered platform deployed at Raven Camp, Greenland, in 2024. Its measurements connect conditions within the snow and firn to the atmosphere above, helping researchers understand the processes accompanying surface melt. [Explore ICECAPS-MELT](https://www.icecapsmelt.org/).
