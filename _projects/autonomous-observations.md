Sustained observations in remote environments require reliable instruments, power, communications and data systems. Developing those capabilities is a research challenge in its own right.

Through [GAMB2LE](https://www.gamb2le.co.uk/), the team works on autonomous observing systems for polar science. The AURORA observatory brings together instrumentation and engineering to support measurements away from conventional research infrastructure.

## An end-to-end approach

We connect the scientific question to the complete observing system: what to measure, how to deploy it, how to monitor performance and how to produce data that researchers can use.

Visit the [GAMB2LE project website](https://www.gamb2le.co.uk/) for current deployments, field updates and the project team.
