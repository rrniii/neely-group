This website introduces the research community around Professor Ryan Neely at the University of Leeds and the National Centre for Atmospheric Science. It is a guide to the science, people and resources, with links to the organisations and records behind them.

## Sources and scope

People's roles are checked against public institutional profiles. The Team page brings together researchers and specialist colleagues; project partnerships and doctoral supervision are described separately from appointments. It is a selection of people involved in the work, rather than a complete staffing directory.

Research summaries link to project and funder records, peer-reviewed papers, data catalogues and public software documentation. Public preprints are labelled separately from published articles. Findings retain the study's setting and limits, and data links point to the original records for coverage, processing, licensing and citation.

The [source and image-credit record](https://github.com/rrniii/neely-group/blob/main/SOURCES.md) documents the evidence used for the site. Ryan's [full CV](https://rrniii.github.io/cv/) provides the wider career record. Private correspondence, calendars and unpublished proposals are not published here.

## Design and reuse

The site builds on [Grau Lab's website](https://github.com/mgrau/graulab.org) and [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio). The original MIT licence and third-party notices are retained. Images have separate credits and reuse conditions in the source record. The two header emblems represent polar atmospheric research and UK BirdCast; they are separate from University and NCAS institutional branding.
