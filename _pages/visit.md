## Research and instrument enquiries

Our main programmes explore [Greenland's ice–atmosphere interface]({{ '/projects/gamb2le/' | relative_url }}), [Antarctic clouds and snowfall]({{ '/projects/tone-ico-snotroll/' | relative_url }}) and [radar aeroecology]({{ '/projects/aeroecology/' | relative_url }}). We also work on radar rainfall, cloud microphysics and the design of observing systems. A short description of your question, the observations you need and any likely field setting will help us connect you with the right expertise.

For access to national observing facilities, consult the [Atmospheric Measurement and Observation Facility](https://amof.ac.uk/) for current instruments and application guidance. Our [data page]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}) links to published observations, and our [teaching page]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}) points to practical training.

## Postgraduate research

Tell us about your interests, relevant experience and the research questions you would like to explore. Formal opportunities and application requirements are published through the [University of Leeds postgraduate research directory](https://phd.leeds.ac.uk/).
