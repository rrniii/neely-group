Our research combines mobile instruments, long-term observations and new platforms for measuring the atmosphere. These photographs show two published examples of that work.

## Mobile weather radar

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/fieldwork/nxpol.jpg" alt="Original NCAS mobile X-band radar at Burn Airfield" class="img-fluid rounded" %}

The original NXPol radar at Burn Airfield, described in [Neely III et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.5194/amt-11-6481-2018), Fig. 1. This is a historical instrument photograph. For current radar capabilities, see the [NCAS Atmospheric Measurement and Observation Facility](https://amof.ac.uk/).

Image © Neely III et al., distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), displayed without alteration.

## Measuring aerosols above the Greenland Ice Sheet

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/fieldwork/helikite.jpg" alt="Helikite with POPS aerosol instrument and Windsond at Summit Station" class="img-fluid rounded" %}

A Helikite carrying aerosol and meteorological instruments during observations at Summit Station, Greenland. The inset identifies the POPS and Windsond payloads. From [Guy et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-24-11103-2024), Fig. 2.

Image © Guy et al., distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), displayed without alteration.
