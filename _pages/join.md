## Postgraduate research

Research can begin with a physical or ecological question, a new measurement approach, or a problem in data analysis. Current [doctoral projects]({{ '/people/#current-doctoral-supervision' | relative_url }}) show how these approaches connect. For polar and atmospheric research ideas, contact Ryan to discuss the scientific fit; use the [Leeds research opportunities directory](https://phd.leeds.ac.uk/) for advertised projects, scholarships and application requirements.

### An advertised route into radar ecology

[Spatio-temporal drivers of insect biodiversity and conservation](https://phd.leeds.ac.uk/project/1666-spatio-temporal-drivers-of-insect-biodiversity-and-conservation) is a School of Biology PhD opportunity covering topics including aeroecology, ecological data science and insect conservation. Christopher Hassall is the lead supervisor; Ryan is listed as an additional supervisor. The advert welcomes applicants from ecology and other disciplines, including computational and mathematical backgrounds.

**Funding: non-funded; applicants need their own funding or a sponsor. Advertised deadline: 30 August 2027.** The listing is open to all nationalities. Contact Christopher through the advert to discuss a project, and follow its application instructions. These details were checked on **11 October 2026**; the University listing is the source for changes.

## Fellowships and research visits

If you are developing an independent fellowship idea, email Ryan with the research question, your experience, the funding scheme and its deadline. An early discussion can establish whether our expertise and facilities fit the proposal. The University's [fellowship guidance](https://researchersupport.leeds.ac.uk/research-funding-and-outputs/fellowships-and-grants/) explains the need to discuss School support and internal selection before applying.

For a shorter collaboration, the [NCAS Visiting Scientist Programme](https://ncas.ac.uk/work-with-us/visiting-scientists/) provides a route to apply for support. Applications are submitted by the NCAS participant and assessed under the programme's criteria. Discuss the purpose, timing and resources for a visit with a potential host before making an application. NCAS also provides an [enquiry route for doctoral placements and co-supervision](https://ncas.ac.uk/study-with-us/phd-supervision-and-placements/), subject to expertise, capacity and project fit.

## Research collaborations

We work with scientists, engineers and data researchers on observation design, instrument development, field measurements and comparison with models. A useful starting point is the uncertainty you want to resolve: what needs measuring, where, at what scale, and how the result will be used.

For existing observations and analysis tools, start with our [data and software resources]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}). For access to national instruments, follow the [Atmospheric Measurement and Observation Facility](https://amof.ac.uk/) application guidance. [NCAS partnerships](https://ncas.ac.uk/work-with-us/) provide further routes for research, commercial and public-sector collaboration.

## How we work together

Instrument engineering, field operations, data analysis and meteorology contribute to the same scientific questions. Ryan's [public research-culture record](https://github.com/rrniii/cv/blob/main/sections/research_culture.md) describes how logistics, safety, data flow and validation are integrated into deployment planning. It also documents the development of researchers into scientific leaders: Heather Guy and Sarah Barr progressed from doctoral research to Research Fellow roles and now share GAMB2LE leadership with Ryan.

[GAMB2LE's core values](https://www.gamb2le.co.uk/about/core-values) make open collaboration, inclusive fieldwork and reduced environmental impact part of that programme's design. For example, AURORA's installation requirements consider a range of physical characteristics. The programme also commits to FAIR and CARE data principles. Our [teaching and training]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}) page links to practical ways to learn the methods behind this work.
