Clare is a doctoral researcher supervised by Ryan Neely since 2026. Her background connects Earth Sciences with practical experience of living and working in Antarctica. [Ryan's public supervision record](https://github.com/rrniii/cv/blob/main/sections/students.md).

## Earth Sciences and Antarctic fieldwork

Clare graduated from Oxford Earth Sciences in 2022. She then joined the UK Antarctic Heritage Trust's four-person team at **Port Lockroy, Goudier Island**, for the 2022–23 Antarctic summer. [Read Oxford's account of her selection](https://www.earth.ox.ac.uk/article/oxford-earth-science-graduate-selected-run-worlds-most-remote-post-office-antarctica).

As **General Assistant and Postmaster**, Clare helped run the historic base and its post office, welcomed visitors and contributed to the team's penguin surveys. The role brought together practical field operations, environmental monitoring and communicating Antarctic history and science to the public.

## Writing from the field

Clare's first-hand account describes the work behind a season at Port Lockroy: caring for the buildings, working around changing weather and sea ice, and surveying penguin colonies on neighbouring islands. It offers a view of the teamwork and practical judgement involved in remote Antarctic fieldwork.
